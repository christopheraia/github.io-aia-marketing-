Bulk Import Lead Sheets

Files are processed in your browser. The system stores only date-level aggregated reporting data + anonymous duplicate fingerprints. Names, emails and phone numbers are not kept in the dashboard. Duplicate rule: the same phone number in the same department and month is counted once.
Study Abroad format: Date, Channel, Campaign M/G. If Counsellor and Country are not inside the file, the selections above are used.
SLICM format: Date, BASE, CAMP DETAILS, Counsellors. SLICM counsellor names are automatically learned from imported rows.
