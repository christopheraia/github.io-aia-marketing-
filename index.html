How general vs country-specific campaigns work

If the campaign name contains wording such as General / All Country, the campaign is stored as a general campaign, while every lead still keeps its own country. For a country-specific campaign, the system uses the lead country for the campaign when it is consistent. This lets the MD dashboard show both campaign-wise and country-wise lead totals correctly.
